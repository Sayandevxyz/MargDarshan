# National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education (Top Class Education for ST Students)

## 1. Scheme Overview
The Top Class Education scheme aims to recognize and promote quality education amongst ST students in premier institutes across India. The scheme covers ST students who secure admission in notified premier institutions (IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, NLUs, etc.).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Category:** Scheduled Tribe (ST) students.
- **Institutions:** Must have secured admission in any of the MoTA notified premier institutions (over 250 notified premier institutions across India).
- **Income Ceiling:** Total family income from all sources must not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per annum.
- **Quota/Ceiling:** Slots are allocated institution-wise each academic year.

## 3. Benefits Covered
- Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges (subject to prescribed institute ceilings).
- Living expenses allowance: ₹3,000/- per month per student.
- Books and stationery grant: ₹5,000/- per annum.
- One-time computer/laptop allowance with accessories: up to ₹45,000/- (once during entire duration of course).

## 4. Mandatory Documents
1. **Valid ST Certificate.**
2. **Family Income Certificate:** Not exceeding ₹6.0 Lakhs per annum.
3. **Institute Admission Letter and Allotment Order.**
4. **Fee Structure Details signed by Registrar or Dean.**
5. **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account.**
