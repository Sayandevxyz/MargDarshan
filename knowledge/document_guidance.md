# Document Guidelines & Image Quality Standards

## Quality Check Criteria
Before submitting documents to MargDarshan:
1. **Clarity & Sharpness:** Ensure text is crisp and legible. Documents with blur score below threshold will be flagged.
2. **Framing & Corners:** Place document flat on a contrasting background. All four corners should be visible.
3. **Lighting & Shadows:** Avoid heavy glare or direct flash shadows on the text or signature.
4. **Resolution:** Minimum recommended image resolution is 1200x1600 px (under 5MB).
5. **Authenticity:** Only upload genuine certificates with valid issuing authority seals, signatures, or digital barcodes.
