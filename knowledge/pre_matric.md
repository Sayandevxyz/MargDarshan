# Pre-Matric Scholarship for ST Students (Classes IX and X)

## 1. Scheme Overview
The Pre-Matric Scholarship is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme implemented through State Governments and UT Administrations. Its objective is to support parents of Scheduled Tribe children for education of their wards studying in classes IX and X, so that the incidence of drop-out, especially in the transition from elementary to secondary stage, is minimized.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Category:** Student must belong to a notified Scheduled Tribe (ST) community.
- **Class of Study:** Must be studying full-time in Class IX or Class X in a Government School, Government-aided School, or School recognized by the Government or Central/State Board of Secondary Education.
- **Family Income:** Annual parental/family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000/- per annum.
- **Single Scholarship:** The scholarship is available for only one stage of study. Students pursuing correspondence or distance courses are not eligible unless specifically notified.

## 3. Financial Assistance
- **Day Scholars:** Monthly scholarship allowance plus book/contingency grant as prescribed under current operational guidelines.
- **Hostellers:** Enhanced maintenance allowance plus annual ad-hoc grant.
- **Disability Add-on:** Additional allowances for students with disabilities as per MoTA norms.

## 4. Required Documents
1. **ST Certificate / Caste Certificate:** Valid certificate issued by a competent revenue authority (Tehsildar/SDM).
2. **Income Certificate:** Valid certificate for the current financial year or self-declaration counter-signed by authorized revenue officer.
3. **Previous Academic Marksheet:** Passing marksheet of Class VIII (for Class IX) or Class IX (for Class X).
4. **Current School Admission Proof / Bonafide Certificate:** Issued by the Principal/Headmaster with school UDISE+ code.
5. **Bank Account Details:** Aadhaar-seeded bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## 5. Verification Workflow
- Level 1: School/Institution Verification via UDISE+ record match.
- Level 2: District Welfare Officer (DWO) / Tribal Development Officer approval.
- Level 3: State Nodal Officer validation and batch generation.
- Level 4: DBT disbursement directly into the student/parent linked bank account.
