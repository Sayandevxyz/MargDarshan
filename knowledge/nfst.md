# National Fellowship for ST Students (M.Phil. / Ph.D.)

## 1. Scheme Overview
National Fellowship for ST Students provides fellowships to Scheduled Tribe students to pursue higher studies leading to M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees in Science, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Engineering/Technology.

## 2. Eligibility
- **Category:** ST Candidates who have qualified UGC-NET / CSIR-NET / GATE examination or equivalent notified criteria.
- **Admission:** Must have registered or gained confirmed admission to regular and full-time M.Phil./Ph.D. courses in recognized Universities / Institutions / Colleges under UGC Act.
- **Income Ceiling:** There is no income ceiling for the fellowship.
- **Tenure:** Up to 2 years for M.Phil. and up to 5 years for Ph.D. (JRF transitioning to SRF after 2-year performance review).

## 3. Fellowship Amount
- **JRF (Junior Research Fellowship):** ₹37,000/- per month.
- **SRF (Senior Research Fellowship):** ₹42,000/- per month.
- **Contingency Grants:** Humanities & Social Sciences (₹10,000 - ₹20,500/year); Sciences/Engineering (₹12,000 - ₹25,000/year).
- **HRA:** As per central government rules based on city classification (X, Y, Z).
- **Escorts/Reader Assistance:** ₹3,000/- per month for physically disabled and visually impaired candidates.
