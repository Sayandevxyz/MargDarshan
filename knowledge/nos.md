# National Overseas Scholarship for ST Candidates (NOS)

## 1. Scheme Overview
The National Overseas Scholarship scheme provides financial assistance to meritorious ST students for pursuing Master's level courses and Ph.D. abroad in prestigious international universities.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Category:** Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates.
- **Minimum Qualification:**
  - For Master's: At least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Bachelor's Degree.
  - For Ph.D.: At least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Master's Degree.
- **Age Limit:** Below 35 years as on first day of the application year.
- **Income Ceiling:** Total family income not exceeding ₹8,00,000/- per annum.
- **Institution Ranking:** Admission in top 500 QS World University Ranked institutions.
- **Child Restriction:** Maximum of two children of the same parents can avail this scholarship.

## 3. Financial Components
- Actual tuition fees and mandatory college expenses directly paid to the university.
- Annual maintenance allowance (USA & other countries: approx $15,400/year; UK: approx £9,900/year).
- Contingency allowance for books, study tour, thesis typing.
- Visa fees, medical insurance premium, and return economy airfare.
