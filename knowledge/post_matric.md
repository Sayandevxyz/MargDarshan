# Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students (Classes XI and above)

## 1. Scheme Overview
The Post-Matric Scholarship is a flagship Centrally Sponsored Scheme providing financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary stages to enable them to complete their higher education.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Category:** Candidate must belong to a notified Scheduled Tribe (ST).
- **Stage of Education:** Must be enrolled in a recognized post-matric course (Class XI, XII, ITI, Polytechnic, Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, M.Phil., Ph.D.).
- **Institution:** Institution must be registered with AISHE (for higher education) or relevant state board.
- **Income Ceiling:** Total family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000/- per annum.
- **One Scholarship Restriction:** A student cannot avail more than one scholarship or fellowship simultaneously from any Government source.

## 3. Financial Component
- Complete non-refundable compulsory course fee reimbursement.
- Monthly maintenance allowance structured across Groups I to IV:
  - Group I: Medical, Engineering, Technology, Planning, Architecture, Agriculture, Veterinary, etc.
  - Group II: Professional graduate and post-graduate courses (Management, Law, Computer Applications, Pharmacy).
  - Group III: General Degree and Diploma courses.
  - Group IV: Classes XI, XII, and vocational training.

## 4. Required Documents
1. **ST Community Certificate:** Competent authority certificate with verified barcode or digital number.
2. **Income Certificate:** Current financial year validity.
3. **Class X / Secondary Marksheet and Qualifying Examination Certificate.**
4. **College/University Admission Letter and Fee Receipt.**
5. **Institution Verification / Bonafide Certificate.**
6. **Active Bank Account Details (DBT enabled via Aadhaar seeding).**
