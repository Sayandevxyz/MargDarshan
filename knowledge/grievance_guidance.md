# Grievance Redressal Guidance

If your application verification is delayed beyond normal processing times, payment has not been credited despite sanction, or you disagree with a verification finding:
1. You can raise a formal Grievance through MargDarshan or directly ask SAATHI AI.
2. Your ticket is routed to the designated District / State Welfare Officer.
3. Standard SLA for grievance acknowledgement is 48 hours, and target resolution is 7-14 working days.
4. Grievance Statuses: Submitted -> Under Review -> Action Required -> Resolved -> Closed.
